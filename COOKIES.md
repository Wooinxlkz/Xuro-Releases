## The short version

**The Xuro website doesn't set any cookies**, and neither does the desktop app. The one optional thing is visit statistics, and nothing is measured unless you allow it.

## On this website

| What | Purpose | Kept for | Needs your consent? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| \`xuro:consent\` in your browser's local storage | Remembers whether you allowed or rejected visit statistics | Up to 12 months | No: it only stores your choice |
| Umami page-view and download counts | Shows us which pages are useful | Until you withdraw or we stop using it | **Yes.** Only after you choose "Allow statistics" |

- **Umami doesn't use cookies.** With your permission it sends the page address, page title, and the download button you clicked, plus basic technical details (like browser and screen size) that Umami uses to produce anonymous statistics.
- **No advertising, social media, or tracking widgets** are embedded in the website. Fonts and images are served from our own domain.
- If visit statistics aren't switched on for the website, you won't see a banner at all, because nothing is measured.

## In the desktop app

The app doesn't use cookies. It keeps settings and layout (and plugin settings, like saved Quran verses) in local storage inside the app on your computer. That data never leaves your computer and isn't shared with anyone.

## Your choices

- **Change your mind any time** with **Cookie settings** in the website footer. Rejecting is as easy as allowing.
- **Clear it yourself** by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
- Your browser can also block or delete storage for any site.
