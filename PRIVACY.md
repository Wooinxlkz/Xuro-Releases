## The short version

- **Your notes stay on your computer.** Xuro is a local-first app. Notes are plain files in a folder you choose. There is no account, and no analytics or tracking in the desktop app.
- **We only receive data if you use Xuro Cloud** (optional publishing). That means your email address and the pages you choose to publish.
- **Some features contact other services** when you use them, such as update checks, library catalogs, link previews, and plugins. The table below lists every one, and what it sees.
- **This website measures visits only if you say yes.** It sets no cookies.
- **We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with data brokers.** No payment details are collected: Xuro Cloud is free while in beta.
- **You can delete your Xuro Cloud account and its data yourself** in the app, or by emailing us.

## What stays on your device

- **Your notes and files** live in the vault folder you pick. Xuro never uploads them unless you publish a note with Xuro Cloud.
- **App data** is kept in a \`.xuro\` folder inside your vault (tasks, bookmarks, and similar) and in the app's local storage on your computer (settings, window layout, plugin settings such as saved Quran verses).
- **PIN locks:** locked notes are encrypted on disk with a key held in your operating system's keychain. We never receive the key or your PIN.
- **A debug log** of unexpected errors is stored on your computer only. Nothing in it is sent anywhere unless you choose to send it to us yourself.

## When the app connects to the internet

The desktop app contacts these services only when the related feature is used. None of them receives your notes.

| Feature | Service | What it can see | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Update check | GitHub (github.com, api.github.com) | Your IP address and the app version | When Xuro checks for updates |
| Bookmarks | The website you bookmark | Your IP address and the address you saved | When you save a bookmark |
| Library search | Open Library, Jikan (MyAnimeList) | Your IP address and your search words | When you search the catalogs |
| Online Manga | MangaDex and its image servers | Your IP address, what you browse and read | When you use Online Manga |
| Xuro Cloud | Xuro's servers on Cloudflare (api.usexuro.app) | Your email address, sign-in codes, and pages you publish | Only if you sign in or publish |
| Quran plugin | Quran.com, AlQuran.cloud, verses.quran.com | Your IP address, which surah and verses you open, and reciter choice | Only if you install and open the plugin |

Cover images and previews are loaded directly from the service that hosts them, so that service also sees your IP address.

## Xuro Cloud

Xuro Cloud is optional. If you sign in and publish, we store:

- **Your email address**, to sign you in. Sign-in works with a one-time code sent by email; there is no password.
- **Sign-in sessions:** a hashed token, kept for 30 days. Sign-in codes expire after 5 minutes.
- **Your published pages:** titles, page content, images, and the links they get. Published pages are **public** to anyone with the link.
- **Storage totals** for the fair-use limit (10 GB per account), and short-lived request records used to limit abuse (a hashed fingerprint, kept for about an hour).
- **Page view counts** for published pages. These count views per page; we don't record who the visitor is. Cloudflare's analytics keeps this for a limited period (currently about three months).

We send one kind of email: the sign-in code you asked for. No marketing email, no newsletter, no welcome message.

## This website

- **No cookies.** The website doesn't set cookies.
- **Visit statistics are optional.** With your permission, Umami counts page views and download clicks without cookies. Nothing is measured unless you choose "Allow statistics", and you can change your mind any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer. See the [Cookie Policy](/cookies).
- **Hosting and downloads.** The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes visitor IP addresses to serve pages and block abuse. The installer is downloaded from GitHub, which also sees your IP address.
- **Fonts and images** are served from our own domain, not from third parties.
